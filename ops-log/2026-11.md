## 2026-11-11 — ComplyKit SEO guide: GDPR vs CCPA comparison

**Batch type:** (a) SEO guide — new long-tail article

**Tool:** complykit

**Rationale:** Deferred tasks from 2026-11-07 (InvoiceQuick 8 guide nav update; ComplyKit 5-6 guide nav update) were found already complete on session start — both included in the same commit as their respective guide creation runs. **Target queries:** "GDPR vs CCPA", "GDPR vs CCPA differences", "does GDPR apply to US companies", "CCPA thresholds", "GDPR consent vs CCPA opt-out", "GDPR CCPA comparison chart", "which applies to me GDPR or CCPA"

**Rationale for this guide:** "GDPR vs CCPA" is one of the highest-volume privacy compliance queries from developers and business operators. ComplyKit generates GDPR/CCPA-aware privacy policies but had no guide explaining the differences between the two laws or which one applies. The comparison is a natural upstream question that leads directly to the generator — a user who learns they need to comply with both wants to generate a policy immediately.

**What shipped:**

- **New guide:** `complykit/guides/gdpr-vs-ccpa.html`  
  Live URL: https://labs.copperbaytech.com/complykit/guides/gdpr-vs-ccpa.html  
  Commit: `2282418` on dukotah/complykit main  
  Word count: ~950 words of original, accurate content  
  Structure:
  - Intro: why the question comes up; not-legal-advice callout
  - Quick comparison table (12 dimensions: full name, applies to, consent model, legal basis, key rights, right to be forgotten, portability, privacy policy requirement, notice at collection, breach notification, fines, enforcement body)
  - Who each law covers: GDPR (no size threshold, territorial reach, processors) vs CCPA/CPRA (for-profit only, 3 thresholds: $25M revenue, 100K consumers, 50% revenue from sale/sharing)
  - Callout: many small sites are GDPR-covered but CCPA-exempt
  - Rights comparison table (8 rights: access, deletion, correction, portability, opt-out of sale, restrict processing, object, non-discrimination; response timeframes)
  - Consent vs opt-out section: GDPR opt-in requirements (freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous); CCPA opt-out model (default allowed; "Do Not Sell or Share" link; GPC signal)
  - 5-step decision framework: which law applies to your business
  - Complying with both simultaneously: 4 CCPA-specific requirements on top of GDPR compliance
  - CTA box linking to the generator
  - 7-question FAQ with TechArticle+FAQPage JSON-LD
  - 4 related guides (which laws apply, GDPR for SaaS, US state laws, do I need a policy)
